## Regulation
DRIFT implements the broadcast (Direct) Remote ID methods of the ASTM F3411 standard:
the Bluetooth 4, Bluetooth 5 Long Range, Wi-Fi Beacon and Wi-Fi NAN transport encodings
of its message set. Regional rules that mandate drone Remote ID reference this standard,
for example the FAA's Means of Compliance (F3586) in the United States and
prEN 4709-002 (Direct Remote ID) in the European Union.

{: .important }
> The above is provided for context only. It is your responsibility to verify that
> your use of this project satisfies the Remote ID requirements of your jurisdiction,
> and that the broadcasts are complete and correct for your aircraft.
